Our lawn comes from Faisalabad mills known for fine thread counts. Perfect for May-September wear. Machine washable. Softens with each wash. Best for: Casual suits, everyday wear, Eid collections.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which fabric is best for Pakistani summer?
Lawn is the classic choice — lightweight, breathable, and washable. For formal summer events, silk georgette offers elegance without the heat.

### Can I wash silk at home?
Plain silk can be hand-washed in cold water with mild detergent. Embroidered silk should always be dry cleaned to protect the threadwork.

### What's the difference between lawn and cambric?
Lawn is lighter (110-130 GSM) and softer, perfect for casual wear. Cambric is crisper (140-160 GSM) and more structured, better for formal pieces.

### Which fabric doesn't wrinkle?
Khaddar and georgette are most wrinkle-resistant. Silk and lawn wrinkle easily but iron smoothly.
